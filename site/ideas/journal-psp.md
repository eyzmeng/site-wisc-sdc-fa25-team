## Pitch

I like to write diaries!

They're usually words, but sometimes I like to add pictures.

![An entry from not so long ago](https://www.rapidcow.org/~ethan/diary.jpg)

I don't usually make entries like these, but you get the point...
pictures are essential to diaries! ;)

There aren't many good diary apps I like.  Often I find them oversaturated
with features that I don't find interesting at best, distracting at worst;
yet they want you to pay subscription for basic features like taking your
data out.  The next best alternative would be to use something less
specialized, like the built-in notes app on your phone, like Apple Notes,
and (it pains me to say this)... OneNote.
(I should not have to explain why neither of these are adequate solutions,
but just for the record, Apple Notes' NoteStore.sqlite3 uses a proprietary
schema that [had to be reverse-engineered](https://github.com/threeplanetssoftware/apple_cloud_notes_parser);
and [OneNote can deny your access to your local copy](https://community.spiceworks.com/t/onenote-has-a-dark-side-stop-using-onenote-until-you-read-this/742229).)

Or use something like [Joplin](https://joplinapp.org/) and [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/).
Like most of our other projects, we are not doing this because we are
out of options, it's more about experience and blah blah... although
in this case, all these options still kind of suck because I miss one
of my favorite journaling apps: [Perspective](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/perspective-a-mindful-journal/id1186753097).
I want to be very specific that this is *not* the AI Journaling app
that somehow had the same name.  (Ugh, app store, could you not
invite apps like that into the living room? >:c)
